Mathematical Disciplines

Core Research & Theoretical Pillars

Analysis

Differential Equations

Analytical investigations into first-order integrating factors, Bernoulli equation coordinate mappings, and the phase-plane geometry of non-linear dynamical systems.

Computing

Numerical Methods

Algorithmic design and error propagation analysis for single-step and multi-stage numerical integrators (Euler and Runge-Kutta RK4) applied to 2 × 2 coupled autonomous systems.

Algebra

Linear Algebra & Vector Spaces

Proof-theoretic frameworks verifying linear independence of vector-valued function sets, coordinate transformations, and Wronskian determinism in functional analysis.

Competition

Putnam Competition Mathematics

Advanced problem-solving methodologies for national and international collegiate competitions, specializing in combinatorial determinants and telescoping recurrences.

About The Author

Advancing Analytical Precision in Mathematics

Hector Martinez is a mathematician and researcher at Clemson University. His work centers on the synthesis of analytical differential equations, numerical ODE solvers, linear algebra, and competition problem solving (including the William Lowell Putnam Mathematical Competition).
