§ Biography

Background & Academic Path

I am an undergraduate in Mathematical Sciences with an emphasis in Computer Science at Clemson University. My work centers on structural mathematics, discrete geometry, and first-principles mathematical exposition.

I focus on clarifying dense algebraic systems into intuitive topological invariants and geometric structures, pairing rigorous proofs with computational models and visualization.

§ Areas of Focus

Mathematical Interests & Exploration

My interests span both pure and applied mathematics. Rather than restricting my work to a single discipline, I focus on first-principles derivations and the underlying structure of whatever problem catches my attention.
